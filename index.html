❓ HELP CENTER

Need help using Danygama Games? Check the information below.

🎮 How do I download a game?

Open Games, choose the game you want, then press VIEW DETAILS. You will see the available download links there. Some games require Coins before links are opened.

🪙 How do Coins work?

Kila siku unaweza kupata 50 FREE Coins mara moja baada ya kuingia kwenye account yako. Kufungua download links za game moja kunahitaji 100 Coins.

💰 How can I buy Coins?

50 Coins ni TSh 200 na 100 Coins ni TSh 500. Malipo yanafanyika kupitia namba 0786538998. Weka payment reference baada ya kulipa. Order yako inahitaji kuthibitishwa kabla Coins kuongezwa.

🔗 What are Part 1, Part 2 and Part 3?

Some large games are divided into several download parts. Download all required parts when a game has multiple links.
